    Readings

    Save, then press Test: every teammate found by name appears here with their health, and their boxes are drawn on the picture.

    How it works

    1. My health bar: drag over your own bar.
    2. Teammates area: drag over the whole list of teammates' names and bars (big enough for a full squad).
    3. Example name and Example health bar: one teammate's name and their bar. This teaches where a bar sits next to a name.

    Teammates are then found by name about once a second, so it doesn't matter who leaves, rejoins or swaps places. A bar that empties and stays empty counts as that player's death, but only while their name is still on screen; someone who leaves doesn't count. Names are matched to Dead-Fest usernames (their gamertags).
