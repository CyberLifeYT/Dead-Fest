    How it works

    Dying Light needs no setup: the teammates list under your health (top left) is watched automatically. For other games, or if your HUD differs, drag the Teammates area over the list of names.

    • Names are found by their shape and trusted once they stay put (scenery moves, the HUD doesn't). Leaving, rejoining and swapping places are fine.
    • Name turns red (Dying Light): a downed teammate stays white; when the name and skull go red they're dead. Counted about 2 seconds later.
    • Who it was: the name is read when it's big enough; otherwise the teammate ticked under Playing with.
    • Needs the Xbox app big enough (maximised is best). Too small, and tracking pauses with a warning rather than guessing.
    • Your own deaths come from the death screen; My health bar is optional and never counts a death on its own.
